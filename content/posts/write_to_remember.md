## Thought
Next to [[write to create]] and [[write to forget]] there is also *write to remember*. At least to remember better. 
Whenever a memory is fresh it can help to write it down, especially good memories like [[20231128 - Coco’s birth]] because in time they will fade. 

### change over time
The way you remember an experience fades and changes over time. You start forgetting the details but maybe more importantly also how you felt about it.
### getting older
I do wonder how this will evolve? Getting older you have more and more distant memories, you of course also create new ones, but what happens to the distant ones? And what memories will stick by you?
