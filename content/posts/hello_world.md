Hello and welcome. This site is my little corner of the internet: part introduction, part logbook. By day I work as a product and engineering consultant in the energy sector through my company TDLX. By night I build things: home automation, small hardware products, self-hosted infrastructure. The [projects](/projects/) page shows what that looks like in practice, and the [experience](/experience/) page covers the professional side.

Why write any of it down? Because most of what I know, I learned from people who bothered to share their half-finished projects and hard-won lessons online. This is my attempt to return the favour. Expect occasional posts about things I built, what broke, and what I learned along the way. If anything here is useful to you, or you just want to talk energy and technology, reach out.
