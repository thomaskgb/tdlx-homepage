> \>_whoami: Building products at the edge of energy & technology. An engineer and product lead who sits between the technology and the people, translating one into the other. Missions rotate, the container stays.

 
## missions (TDLX)
### Sitemark - Senior Product Manager (2026 - now)
The energy infrastructure world is changing fast, and the way we inspect, monitor and maintain it needs to change faster. At Sitemark I work at the intersection of product management and product marketing on their digital twin platform for solar assets, making sure it doesn't just solve problems, but solves the right ones. My focus is simple: deeply understand the market and focus on customer value. In an industry moving at this pace, the gap between a good product and a successful one is often clarity. Clarity of positioning, clarity of purpose, clarity of value. That's where I operate.

### Jetpack.AI - Product Advisor (2026)
A short advisory mission on Strata, an energy management product. I helped sharpen the go-to-market strategy and product direction: mapping the competitive landscape, grounding the product in real-world requirements like ISO 50001, modelling it around concrete energy-buying use cases, and pushing the UI towards the user instead of the data model.

### Elia Group - Innovation Manager e-Mobility (2024 - 2026)
At Elia, the Belgian transmission system operator, I led innovation initiatives to get electric vehicles working *for* the power grid instead of against it. I scouted and collaborated with startups, worked in industry consortia on Vehicle-to-Grid technology, built business cases for flexibility services together with mobility providers, automotive OEMs and charging infrastructure companies, and piloted new technologies for EV grid services. I also got to explore the further-out questions, like what autonomous vehicles could mean for grid flexibility, and represented Elia at industry events such as Enlit Europe.

### Blink Charging - Senior Product Manager (2023 - 2024)
Blink is a US-listed EV charging company that acquired several European charging businesses. Guiding a close-knit team of 15 engineers in Amsterdam and a dedicated remote team in India, I was responsible for bringing Blink's EV Management System, initially developed in the US, to Europe. We often likened it to performing a transplant while the patient is fully awake: integrating the platforms of our new partners in the UK, Belgium and Greece while their day-to-day charging operations kept running.

### Blue Corner - Business Analyst / Product Owner (2022 - 2023)
Blue Corner, a Belgian charging network operator, had just been acquired by Blink. As business analyst and interim product owner I helped the team through the transition, which naturally rolled over into the Blink mission above.

### Eleena - Founder & Director (2019 - 2021)
Eleena, a spinoff of Ecorus, gives social housing tenants and corporations in the Netherlands insight into the performance and returns of their solar installations. We grew to more than 40,000 active customers. I was responsible for the business and technical development, including our monitoring platform which we built in-house using cloud and IoT technologies. Working across so many topics at once only increased my passion for software development.

### SkyOptics - Founder (2015 - 2018)
An after-hours company with my brother, creating some of the first aerial videos for real estate agencies in Belgium. I flew the drones and handled the technology, he took care of the visuals and marketing.

## employee
### Ecorus - Process & IT manager (2018 - 2019)
Ecorus was growing fast, and fast growth breaks processes. I was responsible for standardising how the company worked and building the digital tools to keep control and productivity up, with a hard focus on adoption: a tool nobody uses optimises nothing.

### Ecorus - Project manager (2016 - 2017)
I designed and supervised the build of utility grade solar power plants, managing budget, planning and quality. Most memorable project: the Nike distribution centre in Laakdal, more than 8,000 panels and at the time the largest project of its kind in Flanders since the green certificate subsidies ended.

### Ecorus - Project engineer (2014 - 2016)
The Solar Team showed me the beauty of clean energy, so I went looking for the same driven, ambitious atmosphere in industry and found it at Ecorus, engineering and supporting large solar projects.

### Punch Powertrain Solar Team - Business relations & finance (2012 - 2014)
As a member of the Belgian Solar Team I was responsible for sponsoring and finances: we contacted more than 300 companies to fund the build of a solar car and race it through the Australian outback. With 17 team members we finished 6th in the World Solar Challenge and won the innovation award. I still look back on it as the project that started everything, and I stayed on as an advisor afterwards, helping the 2017 team reach an incredible 3rd place.
